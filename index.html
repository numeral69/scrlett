About

Scrlett is a modern shoegaze-inspired project built on texture, atmosphere, and emotional intensity. Their sound blends layered guitars with melodic fragility, creating immersive, cinematic compositions.

The project explores contrast — distortion and clarity, chaos and stillness — forming a sonic identity rooted in mood and space.
