About

Noise, distortion,
and the space between.

Scrlett emerged from late nights and heavy reverb — a band built on the belief that sound should feel like something. Rooted in the shoegaze tradition, their music layers walls of guitar noise over fragile melodies, finding beauty in the blur.

Drawing from the lineages of My Bloody Valentine, Slowdive, and Lush, Scrlett carve out their own sonic territory — dense, cinematic, and achingly intimate.

Their debut album Velvet Static is out now on all platforms.
