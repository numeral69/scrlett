ABOUT

SCRLETT is a shoegaze band known for their immersive soundscapes and emotionally charged melodies.

They craft dreamy, layered textures that draw listeners in and create a captivating live experience.

Their performances blend atmospheric intensity with raw emotion, leaving audiences lost in the moment.

With a passion for sonic exploration and heartfelt expression, SCRLETT connects deeply through both music and performance.
